name: zb-feedback
description: Turn Alex's test notes or screenshots into ZibaldOne cards. Use when Alex sends test findings.
---

1. Split the notes into one finding each.
2. Fill the card template: Versione · Dispositivo · Passi · Atteso · Cosa è successo · Screenshot.
3. Show the list to Alex.
4. Only after his ok, create the cards in Inbox, tag them with the date, letter C and number.
